Aftercare Instructions

How to keep your new tattoo looking its best.

Important: Everyone's skin heals differently. These are general guidelines. If you have concerns about how your tattoo is healing, contact me.

First 24 Hours

  1. Leave the bandage on. Keep the protective covering on for the time recommended during your session (typically 2–4 hours, or overnight if using a second-skin bandage).
  2. Wash gently. After removing the bandage, wash with unscented soap and lukewarm water. Use your hand only—no cloth or sponge. Pat dry with a clean paper towel.
  3. Apply aftercare product. Use a thin layer of fragrance-free moisturizer or specialized tattoo aftercare product.
  4. Let it breathe. Don't re-bandage unless instructed. Tattoos heal best with air circulation.

Days 2–14 (Healing Phase)

  • Wash 2–3 times daily with unscented soap and warm water. Use your hands, gently.
  • Dry completely with a clean paper towel (cloth can harbor bacteria).
  • Moisturize immediately after washing while skin is still slightly damp.
  • Keep it uncovered when at home. Exposure to air speeds healing.
  • Wear clean, loose clothing around the tattoo to avoid friction.
  • Sleep on clean sheets changed regularly.

What to Avoid

  • 🚫 Don't pick, scratch, or peel—no matter how itchy.
  • 🚫 Avoid prolonged sun exposure for at least 2–4 weeks.
  • 🚫 No swimming, hot tubs, or soaking for 2 weeks.
  • 🚫 Don't use scented lotions, oils, or perfumes on the tattoo.
  • 🚫 Avoid tight or synthetic clothing over the area.
  • 🚫 No heavy exercise or excessive sweating for 1–2 weeks.
  • 🚫 Don't submerge in water (showers are fine; baths aren't).

Long-Term Care

Once healed (2–4 weeks), continue protecting your tattoo:

  • Use sunscreen (SPF 30+) whenever the tattoo is exposed to the sun.
  • Keep skin moisturized to maintain color vibrancy.
  • Avoid excessive sun exposure to prevent fading.
  • Stay hydrated and maintain healthy skin.
