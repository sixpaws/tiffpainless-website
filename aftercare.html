Aftercare

Everything you need to know to keep your new tattoo looking fresh

Your tattoo is an open wound. Proper aftercare is essential to prevent infection and ensure your tattoo heals beautifully. Follow these instructions carefully.

First 24 Hours

  • Leave the bandage on for 2-4 hours
  • Gently wash with unscented soap and warm water
  • Pat dry with a clean towel (don't rub)
  • Apply a thin layer of fragrance-free moisturizer
  • Do NOT re-bandage after the initial removal

Days 2-14

  • Wash 2-3 times daily with unscented soap and lukewarm water
  • Apply fragrance-free moisturizer after each wash
  • Keep the tattoo uncovered and exposed to air when possible
  • Wear loose, clean clothing over the tattoo
  • Sleep on clean sheets
  • Avoid direct sunlight and tanning beds
  • Avoid soaking in hot tubs, pools, or taking baths

Things to Avoid

  • Do NOT scratch or pick at the tattoo
  • Do NOT expose to direct sunlight for at least 2-3 weeks
  • Do NOT soak in water (baths, pools, hot tubs)
  • Do NOT apply petroleum-based products
  • Do NOT wear tight clothing over the tattoo
  • Do NOT exercise or do heavy sweating for at least 1 week
  • Do NOT apply cologne, perfume, or scented lotions
Seek medical attention if: Your tattoo shows signs of infection (increased redness, warmth, pus, or excessive swelling), you develop a fever, or you have any other concerns about your healing tattoo.

Long-Term Care

After your tattoo heals (2-4 weeks), continue to protect it:

  • Apply sunscreen (SPF 30+) whenever the tattoo is exposed to sun
  • Keep skin moisturized to maintain tattoo vibrancy
  • Avoid prolonged sun exposure, especially on new tattoos
  • Stay hydrated and maintain healthy skin
