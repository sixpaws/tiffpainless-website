Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about getting a tattoo

You can submit your inquiry through our contact form or reach out directly. Let us know the design you're interested in, placement, and your availability. We'll get back to you as soon as possible to confirm your appointment.
Pricing varies based on the complexity, size, and placement of your design. We offer custom pricing for each unique project. Please contact us with details about your desired tattoo for an accurate quote.
We typically work by appointment only to ensure the best experience for each client. Walk-ins may be accommodated depending on availability, but we recommend booking in advance.
Get plenty of rest, eat a good meal before your appointment, and avoid alcohol the night before. Wear comfortable clothing and bring any references or sketches you'd like to discuss. Don't worry if you're nervous—we've worked with first-time clients before!
The duration depends on the size and complexity of your design. A small tattoo might take 30 minutes to an hour, while larger pieces could take several hours. We'll provide an estimate during your consultation.
