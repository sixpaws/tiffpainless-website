Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

Head over to the Book an Appointment page and fill out the inquiry form. Include as much detail as possible — design idea, placement, size, and any reference images. You'll receive a response within 2–3 business days to discuss details and scheduling.

Pricing depends on the size, detail, placement, and time required. Smaller pieces are typically quoted at a flat rate, while larger work is priced at an hourly rate. You'll receive a quote or estimate during the consultation process.

Yes, a deposit is required to secure your appointment and cover design time. The deposit is non-refundable but is applied toward the final cost of your tattoo. Deposits may be transferred to a new date if you reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice.

Eat a good meal before your session, stay hydrated, and get plenty of rest the night before. Avoid alcohol and blood-thinning medications for 24 hours prior. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being tattooed. Bring a valid ID.

Appointments are preferred to ensure quality time for each piece. Occasional flash days and walk-in events are announced on social media — follow along for updates.

Absolutely! Reference images and your own artwork are welcome. They help guide the design process. Keep in mind that exact copies of another artist's tattoo are not reproduced — your design will be custom-adapted to make it uniquely yours.

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice if you need to reschedule. Cancellations within 48 hours or no-shows will forfeit their deposit. To reschedule, use the contact form as early as possible.

You must be 18 years or older with a valid government-issued photo ID. No exceptions — this is New Jersey state law.

Yes, one free touch-up is included within 3 months of your appointment, provided you followed the aftercare instructions. Touch-ups after 3 months or due to poor aftercare may incur a fee.
